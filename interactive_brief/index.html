The insight that changes the offer mix

Concierge is not “more of the same.” It helps new users and can hurt veterans.

Population averages (~+1.6pp for concierge) hide a sign flip. That is why a one-size offer — or a churn-ranked concierge blast — funds sleeping dogs.

What Product should take from this

Heavy-touch saves are a scarce instrument. Use them where tenure and engagement suggest the subscriber is persuadable — not where they are simply high-risk.

Cheap nudges win most of the value-per-dollar allocations. Concierge appears sparingly in the shipped scoring mix (61 of 40,000 users) because that is where the economics clear the bar.

Recommendations

What to do with this on Monday.

For Finance

Fund retention offers against incremental net retained revenue, with a do-nothing and a churn-targeting baseline on every readout. At half budget, re-optimize — our $20k re-solve still delivered about +$105k DR incremental on holdout, showing diminishing returns as spend grows.

For Product

Default save journeys should not escalate every at-risk user to human outreach. Prefer scalable nudges; reserve concierge for segments where experiments show positive lift (newer subscribers, in this data). Treat “do nothing” as a first-class treatment.

For Data / ML

Block post-treatment features in the feature store. Keep a randomized holdout or exploration slice so effect estimates stay honest after launch. Separate the ranking score used for monitoring from the budgeted allocation objective.

For Leadership

The win is not a fancier model — it is refusing to pay sleeping dogs and forcing every offer through a value-minus-cost gate under a real constraint. That is the operating principle to keep as the subscription portfolio grows across In Tandem brands.
